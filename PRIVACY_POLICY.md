# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026

**Corsairs.lol** ("Corsairs," "we," "us," or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information in connection with your use of the Corsairs.lol mobile application, web application, and related services (the "Application").

As a decentralized, non-custodial Web3 application, **Corsairs.lol does not collect, store, or sell personal identifying information** (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, or physical addresses).

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We are built on principles of minimal data collection:
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not require accounts, logins, passwords, emails, or personal details to browse or interact with the app.
- **No Custody of Private Keys:** We never request, collect, store, or have access to your wallet private keys, seed phrases, or credentials.
- **No Financial Tracking:** We do not collect credit card numbers, bank account details, or traditional payment info.

---

## 2. Information Handled & Processed

### 2.1 Public Blockchain Data
When you connect your wallet via Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) and perform transactions:
- **Public Wallet Addresses:** Your public Solana address is read by the app to display your balance, active territories, and transaction eligibility.
- **On-Chain Transactions:** Purchases, custom bids, payouts, and transaction signatures are broadcast directly to the public Solana blockchain ledger. **All public blockchain records are immutable and publicly accessible to anyone.**

### 2.2 User-Submitted Banner Content
When you claim a territory, any custom text you choose to provide (such as a Banner Title, Tagline, and External URL) is published to the public territory indexer and visible to all users browsing the world map.

### 2.3 Device & Local Storage
The Application utilizes your device’s local storage (`AsyncStorage`) exclusively to enhance your experience:
- Caching recent territory data and map states for high-performance offline rendering.
- Saving your local application preferences (e.g., sound/haptics toggles, notification settings).
- Storing local draft edits for your banners before you submit them on-chain.
- Managing local device push notifications for outbid alerts.

*This data remains on your physical device and is not exported to third-party advertising brokers.*

### 2.4 Server & API Interactions
To deliver real-time map synchronization and intent preparation:
- **API Requests:** When fetching the live territory board or requesting transaction intents, standard non-identifying technical metadata (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) may be processed temporarily by our infrastructure (e.g., Supabase Edge Functions) solely for DDoS mitigation, rate limiting, and server diagnostic logs.
- **No Ad Tracking:** We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, data brokers, or user profiling cookies.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The Application interfaces with external decentralized services and infrastructure providers:
- **Solana Network & RPC Nodes:** Your wallet interactions and balance checks communicate with decentralized Solana RPC nodes (e.g., Solana Mainnet-Beta RPC). These nodes process transactions according to their respective network protocols.
- **Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA):** Wallet authorization and signing happen entirely within your chosen independent wallet application (such as Phantom, Solflare, Seed Vault, or other MWA-compatible apps). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party wallet providers.
- **External Links:** User banners may contain links to external third-party websites. Clicking on these links directs you to third-party domains governed by their own privacy policies.

---

## 4. Data Security

Because we do not store personal data, passwords, or private keys on our servers, the primary security of your digital assets rests in the protection of your non-custodial wallet and private keys. We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all client-to-server communications and API endpoints.
