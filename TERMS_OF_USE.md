## 1. Description of the Service

Corsairs.lol is an Android-first, decentralized territory market and advertising billboard interface built on the Solana blockchain. The Application allows users to:
- Connect non-custodial Solana wallets via the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) or standard Web3 providers.
- Claim, bid on, and acquire virtual territories representing countries on a live world map.
- Publish customizable digital banners, messages, taglines, and links associated with held territories.
- Participate in on-chain game mechanics where subsequent territory takeovers trigger automated payout splits and revenue shares in accordance with the protocol rules.
